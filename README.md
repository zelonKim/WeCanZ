# 🌎 WeCanZ - We Can be Gen-Z

> **Connect Beyond Borders, Live the Gen-Z Vibe**  
> WeCanZ is a new kind of social meetup platform designed for Gen-Z to discover, host, and join local hangouts, cultural experiences, and spontaneous gatherings across the US, UK, and South Korea—breaking boundaries of nationality and age. By welcoming anyone young at heart who wants to experience the Gen-Z lifestyle, it bridges generations and brings people of all ages together.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Z-Meetup Discovery & Joining:** Browse and participate in Gen-Z hangouts, hobby sessions, and cultural events matching your vibe.
- **Meetup Hosting:** Create a new Meetup in seconds with location details, max participant limits, and category tags.
- **Friend Connections:** Add friends, build your social network, and keep track of fellow participants.
- **Real-Time DM Messaging:** Coordinate plans smoothly with hosts and guests via instant WebSocket-powered in-app direct messaging.
- **Z-Spot Exploration:** Discover trending local spots and popular hangout zones favored by Gen-Z.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

WeCanZ is built with a modern, high-performance, and type-safe full-stack architecture to deliver a seamless experience across Web and Mobile.

### Backend

- **Framework:** NestJS 11
- **Real-Time:** WebSockets (instant messaging and notifications)
- **Database & ORM:** Prisma / PostgreSQL / NeonDB

### Frontend

- **Web App:** Next.js 15
- **Mobile App:** React Native (Expo SDK 55)
- **Styling & State:** Tailwind CSS (Web) / StyleSheet (Mobile) / TanStack Query
